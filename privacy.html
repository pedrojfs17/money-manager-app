Money Manager

Privacy policy

Last updated 28 September 2026

The short version

Money Manager keeps your financial data on your phone. There is no Money Manager account and no Money Manager server. The developer collects nothing: no analytics, no tracking, no advertising, and no copy of your records.

What stays on your phone

Accounts, records, categories, rules, investments and settings are stored in a local database on the device. Passphrases, bank keys and AI keys are kept in the phone's secure storage. Uninstalling the app deletes all of it.

Optional features that talk to other services

These are off until you turn them on, and each one uses your own account with that service. Data goes straight from your phone to the service, never through the developer.

  • Google Drive backup. Backups are encrypted on the phone with AES-256-GCM using your passphrase before they are uploaded, so Google cannot read them. See the Google section below.
  • Bank sync. Uses Enable Banking with credentials you create. The app receives your bank accounts, balances and transactions from Enable Banking and stores them on the phone.
  • AI categorisation. Sends the merchant name, whether the money came in or went out, and your category names to the AI provider you choose (Anthropic, OpenAI or Google), using your own API key. Amounts, dates, accounts and notes are never sent.
  • Investment prices. Sends the ticker symbols of your investments to Yahoo Finance to fetch prices. Nothing else is sent.
  • Payment capture (Android). Reads notifications from the payment app you pick, on the phone, to create records. The notifications are not sent anywhere.
  • Your own service. If you run your own Money Manager service, the phone sends it a read-only copy of your data. You host it, so it is yours.

Google user data

Google Drive backup asks for one permission, drive.file. It lets the app see and change only the files it created itself, in a folder called "Money Manager backups". The app cannot see anything else in your Drive.

The app uses this access only to upload, list, download and remove its own encrypted backups. The data is not sent to the developer, not shared with anyone, not used for advertising and not used to train AI models.

Money Manager's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

To stop it, disconnect Google Drive in the app, or remove Money Manager at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Backups already in your Drive stay there until you delete them.

Children

Money Manager is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new date.

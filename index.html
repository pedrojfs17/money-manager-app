No account. No server of ours. Just your phone.

There is nothing to sign up for. Everything you record is stored on the phone, and nobody else gets a copy, including us.

  • Works offline

    Airplane mode changes nothing. Records, stats and backups to a file all work without a connection.

  • Encrypted backups

    A daily backup to your own Google Drive, encrypted on the phone with a passphrase only you know. Google cannot read it.

  • Nothing collected

    No analytics, no ads, no tracking. Bank, AI and backup connections use your own accounts and go straight from your phone.

  • Your own server, if you want

    Run the optional service for a read-only view in the browser, and an MCP server so your AI assistant can answer questions about your money.

And the small things that make it yours.

  • Categories and subcategoriesSupermarket and Bakery roll up into Groceries.
  • LabelsTags that cut across categories, like Holidays or Family.
  • Your coloursTwelve colours and an icon for every account, category and label.
  • Filters that find itSearch by text, amount, date range, account, category or label.
  • Hide amountsOne switch turns every figure into dots, for checking in public.
  • Light and darkFollows your phone, or pick one yourself.
  • Accounts in your orderEvery list and picker follows the order you set.
  • Any currencyEach account keeps its own currency, with nothing converted behind your back.
  • New phone, same moneyRestore a backup from a file or from Drive on the first screen.
